Create an AI-designed web memorial to my grandparents, Lester and Winnie Bradford, who led fascinating careers in tropical agroforestry and medicine, respectively, in countries around the world including Sierra Leone, Pakistan, and Sudan.

It's a public website that starts out totally blank.

The process of changes once the site is live is as follows:
- The project is open-ended, but based at all times on publicly available information about my grandparents 
- Photos are a necessity. You can also use videos that have been added to this folder, but everything else should be from the public internet.
- Apart from the focus on my grandparents' lives, the project is open-ended.
- When I run an agent session in this directory locally and type "Continue", the agent examines the existing website and asks itself: "What can I do to make this website more beautiful, detailed, compelling, engaging, polished, unique, performant, subtle, surprising, mobile-friendly, reliable, secure, cohesive, intriguing, well designed, and technically impressive? Does it honor the memory of these two people, and paint a clear picture of who they were and what they did? What are the bugs I can identify and fix? What would draw more people in to increase the reach of the site? What is my vision for the future of this project?" It then implements one or more specific, contained sets of changes and publishes them.
